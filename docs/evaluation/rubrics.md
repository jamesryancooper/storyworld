# Human evaluation rubrics (B4 — definitions)

> Definitions only, per DEC-0012: model-assisted scoring stays mocked until
> the owner supplies provider keys; human panels use these rubrics at V1
> and beyond. Every rubric scores 1–5 with anchored endpoints; a scored
> item always references the exact content hashes evaluated.

## R1 — Continuity fidelity
Does the artifact contradict the pinned canon release?
1 = direct contradiction of hard canon (wrong state at the scene's story
time, impossible knowledge, broken locked attribute). 3 = no hard
contradiction; soft-canon or ambiguity concerns noted. 5 = every
canon-derivable detail is consistent, including locked attributes and
story-time-derived state.

## R2 — Character consistency
Do depicted characters match their accepted canonical descriptions and
current story-time state? 1 = unrecognizable or state-contradicting.
3 = recognizable with minor drift outside locked attributes. 5 = faithful
in appearance, state, and voice.

## R3 — Narrative coherence
Does the unit serve its declared scene purpose and emotional objective?
1 = incoherent or contradicts the structure's thread state. 3 = coherent
but flat against the declared objective. 5 = clearly advances the unit's
purpose; reveals honor earliest-permitted constraints.

## R4 — Placement naturalness (commerce campaigns)
Product presence per the campaign brief. 1 = claim violation, prohibited
depiction, or jarring placement. 3 = compliant but conspicuous.
5 = compliant, natural, and story-integrated; only approved facts/claims.

## R5 — Rights and brand safety
1 = uses restricted source material or violates required disclosures.
3 = compliant with disclosure debt worth flagging. 5 = fully compliant;
provenance complete and disclosures present.

## Protocol
- Two independent raters per item; disagreement > 1 point escalates to
  the owner. Scores attach to continuity findings as human-layer evidence
  and never overwrite deterministic findings.
- Baseline gate (V1): no released unit below 4 on R1/R5; campaign items
  additionally no below 4 on R4.
