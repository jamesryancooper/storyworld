## Context

Ten working titles await names; established names (including Dumpster Fire Friends, whose Garbage Pail Kids parody adjacency needs care) were never screened. AI-assisted works need documented human creative control to be registrable, and Storyworld already records the needed events (briefs, direction, selection, revision, acceptance) without compiling them. Family collaborators contribute stories, photographs, and potentially voice with no agreements. The entity/umbrella structure is an undecided one-line concept.

## Decision (proposed)

1. Adopt the naming/clearance pipeline (candidates → knockout search with recorded evidence → clearance decision → registration decision tracked in rights) as part of property creation; run the first-pass screen across the working titles and established names.
2. Adopt the **authorship dossier** as a platform derivative per release (compiled human creative acts, registration-ready) plus AI-contribution disclosure fields; pilot on the fairytale book.
3. Require contributor agreements (ownership/license, attribution, revocation handling, parental consent for minors) stored as rights-layer consent records before contributions are accepted.
4. Decide the entity/umbrella structure with counsel; record the owning entity per property and registration in the rights layer.
5. Adopt the AI-transparency stance as versioned brand policy: human-authored stories, AI-assisted production, disclosed plainly per property class; never present AI-generated depictions of real people or family moments as real recordings; channel AI-labeling automated through adaptors; properties may tighten but never loosen the floor.
