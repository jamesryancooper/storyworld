## Context

The taxonomy separates narrative substance (families, engines, structures, arcs, knowledge frames) from realization (media/channel classes, format and length ladders per medium, release/seriality models, realization styles, presentation modes), plus audience roles and continuity patterns, composed by a 12-part formula. The building-blocks document carries the craft catalogs (engines, structures, emotional arcs, knowledge frames). The continuity patterns map one-to-one onto the platform's pattern fixtures and probes.

## Decision (proposed)

1. Adopt both documents as canonical product-definition material and register them as supporting artifacts.
2. Adopt the substance/realization separation and the composition formula as the platform's classification grammar; templates express taxonomy selections as configurable vocabulary.
3. Adopt the three-way derivation distinction (derivation vs. adaptation vs. transmedia complementary canon) as governing language, aligned with the corresponding platform patterns.
4. Adopt the taxonomy's dimension-to-platform mapping table as the authoritative crosswalk (families→templates, styles→art-style definitions, formats→adaptors/renditions, continuity→pattern fixtures).
