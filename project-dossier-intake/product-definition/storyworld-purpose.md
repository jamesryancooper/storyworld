## Purpose

**Storyworld brings any creator’s ideas and vision to life.**

It serves as a calm, intuitive creative partner—simple by default and powerful by choice. Storyworld gives creators one clear path from inspiration to finished work, communicates in familiar creative language, and makes it easy to understand what is official, what remains in development, what needs attention, and what comes next.

Behind the scenes, it manages the technical complexity required to make creative work dependable, traceable, recoverable, and coordinated. Everyday creation remains fast and forgiving, while consequential decisions involving canon, masters, rights, releases, and publication are deliberate, protected, and clearly explained.

As creators’ needs grow, Storyworld progressively reveals greater precision and control without imposing that complexity on everyone. The result is an experience that is effortless for common creative work, precise when needed, and powerful without being overwhelming.

## Interpretation boundary

“Any creator” expresses an inclusive design aspiration. It does not promise current access, external availability, support for every creator or medium, production readiness, or unrestricted product scope.

Recording this target direction does not accept a successor decision or authorize implementation, spending, deployment, publication, external communication, external access, or product rollout.
