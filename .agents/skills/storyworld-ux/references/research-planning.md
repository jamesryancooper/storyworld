# Usability research planning

Use this reference to plan creator studies, interview guides, task scenarios,
or synthesis. Do not recruit, contact, schedule, record, compensate, or collect
participant data without separate authorization.

## Keep evidence honest

Use the evidence labels in `SKILL.md`. A heuristic review, agent walkthrough,
fixture, persona, or predicted reaction is not user research or participant
evidence. Never invent quotations, task success, prevalence, confidence,
completion time, ease scores, metrics, or consensus.

## Define the decision first

State:

1. Product decision the study will inform.
2. Creator segment, experience, job, and critical journey.
3. Known observed facts and current V1 alpha limits.
4. Hypotheses and plausible counter-hypotheses.
5. Evidence that would change the decision.
6. Canon, release, publishing, privacy, accessibility, or authority risks.

Use the lowest-fidelity artifact that can answer the question: task
walkthrough, prototype, local synthetic build, or current alpha. Do not use
fake authority, publication, provider effects, or real creator material.

## Recruit purposefully

Define relevant characteristics rather than “generic users,” such as:

- First-time versus experienced serialized creator, showrunner, editor,
  continuity reviewer, art producer, or interactive-world designer.
- Solo versus small-team workflow.
- Experience maintaining characters, places, rules, timelines, arcs, assets,
  and release approvals.
- Familiarity with generative tools and expectations about AI authority.
- Accessibility needs relevant to keyboard, screen reader, zoom, low vision,
  cognitive load, or motor access.
- Exclusion criteria, conflicts, and prior Storyworld exposure.

Qualitative sessions can reveal and explain failure modes; they do not
estimate prevalence without an appropriate study design. State sample and
recruitment limits and do not prescribe a universal participant count.

## Design neutral, realistic tasks

- Give a creative scenario and goal, not interface steps or feature names.
- Use synthetic, non-sensitive world data.
- Include the consequential decision and recovery path, not only the happy
  path.
- Ask the participant to distinguish suggestion/draft, model proposal,
  accepted canon, pinned production, release, and publication.
- Include one continuity contradiction and one relevant permission, stale,
  unavailable, or ambiguous outcome.
- Observe before probing. Ask what the participant noticed, expected, and
  believed would happen without endorsing an answer.
- Never ask participants to use real credentials, private manuscripts,
  personal source material, provider accounts, production systems, or publish
  anything.

## Produce a complete plan

Include:

- Research decision, questions, hypotheses, and counter-hypotheses.
- Participant criteria, recruitment approach, sample rationale, and limits.
- Consent, privacy, recording, retention, compensation, and accessibility
  accommodations for operator review.
- Method, fidelity, environment, and synthetic/alpha disclosure.
- Neutral task scenarios and order controls when comparing variants.
- First-click/orientation, unguided task completion, think-aloud observation,
  post-task self-report, and concrete comprehension checks where applicable.
- Moderator introduction, neutral probes, and stop/safety rules.
- Notes template separating behavior, participant words, researcher
  interpretation, severity, and evidence location.
- Measures defined before sessions: task outcome, critical errors, recovery,
  wrong turns, time only when meaningful, comprehension, confidence
  self-report, and access barriers. Do not invent targets or baselines.
- Synthesis method, contradictory-evidence handling, traceability, decision
  rules, iteration plan, limitations, and remaining uncertainty.

## Synthesize only supplied evidence

Preserve:

- What the participant did.
- What the participant said.
- Researcher interpretation.
- Recommended product response.

Retain contradictions and outliers. Report counts only from supplied records
and do not generalize beyond the sample. A single observation can expose a
severe failure mode without proving frequency.

For planning-only work, end with hypotheses and an evidence-capture protocol,
not fabricated findings.
