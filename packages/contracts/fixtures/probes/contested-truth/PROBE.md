# PROBE-CONTESTED-TRUTH — contested-truth

Source: owner pattern program, section B (Multiple points of view and contested truth).

Generic capability tested: assertions/testimony as first-class records distinct from accepted canon facts; honesty, accuracy, and confidence as independent axes; speaker knowledge basis at testimony time; POV visibility.

Belongs in the reusable domain model: the assertion record (speaker, claim, story-time, knowledge basis, honesty, accuracy, confidence, visibility, status/supersession) on canon releases; creator truth as secret_truth facts separate from audience-visible facts.

Template/policy/adapter-specific: edition-scoped reveal policies (which edition may surface a narrator's claim), interview formats, unreliable-narrator styling.

Must-tests carried: said vs believes vs true remain distinguishable; honesty vs accuracy independent (Brill lies yet is accurate); uncertainty explicit; mechanical records are evidence, not resolution.
Deferred to F3 kernel: automatic contradiction detection between assertions and facts; per-edition reveal permission enforcement.
