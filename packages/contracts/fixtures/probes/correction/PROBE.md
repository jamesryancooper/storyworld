# PROBE-CORRECTION — correction

Source: owner pattern program, section G (Evidence-based nonfiction and post-publication correction).

Generic capability tested: claims linked to evidence, conflicting sources, explicit confidence, typed corrections that supersede without erasing, preserved publication records.

Belongs in the reusable domain model: assertion evidence_refs and confidence; supersession with typed correction_type; publication preservation as facts plus the media lifecycle's published->superseded path (never deletion).

Template/policy/adapter-specific: fact-check workflows, correction-notice formats, editorial standards for 'probable' language.

Must-tests carried: five claims with evidence; disputed date reported as disputed; probable marked probable; correction preserves the superseded assertion with successor link (MET-F1 correct_assertion targets this file); quote carries its excerpt-permission limitation.
Deferred to later phases: generated-caption certainty enforcement and downstream derivative invalidation (B3/B4 metamorphic families); quote-rights blocking (rights-evidence integration).
