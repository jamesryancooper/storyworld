## Decision-handling rule

A schema conversion must preserve the distinction among:

1. source material that was retrieved;
2. accepted production canon;
3. fixture-only canon;
4. creator-only truth;
5. public story knowledge;
6. restricted fictional source material;
7. policy or safety constraints that govern production but are not facts inside the story.

Flattening these into one undifferentiated “canon” collection would make several of the fixtures pass structurally while failing their actual purpose.

## Review basis

The packet was prepared from the accessible Storyworld repository and its canonical dossier/contract charter, the saved dossier in the user’s Library, and recovered project-specific conversation context. The shared conversation page itself was not retrievable in the working browser, so no claim is made that every historical message was read verbatim. Where retrieval did not establish an exact creative detail, the packet labels the completion as a new fixture decision instead of presenting it as recovered canon.
