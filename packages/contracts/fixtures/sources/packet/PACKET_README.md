# Storyworld Fixture Authoring Packet

**Status:** Author-proposed creative source material for repository fixtures  
**Prepared for:** Storyworld F1 contract modeling and extended golden-corpus coverage  
**Format:** Raw creative decisions, intentionally more structured than prose but not bound to a final schema

## Purpose

This packet supplies rights-safe creative decisions that can be converted into formal Storyworld fixture records. It covers the four fixtures required by the current F1 contract plan and five additional narrative-property fixtures that broaden regression coverage.

The four F1 fixture families are already named by the repository contract charter:

1. Stillhouse Archive mini-season
2. Editorial carousel
3. BeKindRewind world slice
4. Commerce Foundry campaign

The extended fixtures cover:

5. Dumpster Fire Friends
6. Notes to My Daughter
7. Living Inheritance
8. Proper Manhood
9. The Lanterns

## Source-status legend

Every substantive choice is governed by one of these labels:

- **ESTABLISHED PROPERTY CANON** — recovered from prior user-owned project decisions.
- **FIXTURE CANON DECISION** — a new, rights-safe decision authored specifically to make the fixture complete and testable.
- **RESTRICTED TEST SOURCE** — wholly invented private-source material used only to test access control, source separation, and safe public transformation.

Accepting a **FIXTURE CANON DECISION** into the fixture repository makes it fixture canon only. It does not silently revise the production canon of a commercial or creative property. Promotion from fixture canon into production canon should require a separate explicit author decision.

## Rights and safety rules applied throughout

- All people, organizations, products, businesses, addresses, documents, quotations, and events introduced in this packet are fictional.
- No real person’s private history, likeness, message, journal, diagnosis, address, workplace, or identifying event is used.
- No real commercial brand, product, film, game, song, character, or franchise appears in fixture content.
- Source-note examples are invented and exist solely to exercise restricted-source handling.
- Any resemblance to real people or organizations is coincidental and unintended.
- Public outputs must preserve the restrictions stated inside each fixture.

## Why the additional fixtures are not redundant

- The generic editorial fixture proves restricted-source separation without tying the test to a named property.
- Notes to My Daughter adds unusually strict dignity, likeness, and indirect-identification controls.
- Living Inheritance adds original-photo rights, long-lived editorial taxonomy, and cross-format derivation.
- Proper Manhood adds a repeatable lesson taxonomy, fixed visual tokens, series numbering, and cross-references.
- Dumpster Fire Friends adds collection membership, stable card identity, character statistics, reusable layouts, and behavior-to-recovery structure.
- The Lanterns adds hard world rules, character belief versus creator truth, moral-choice dependencies, and a safety policy that must constrain generation without becoming narrative canon.
