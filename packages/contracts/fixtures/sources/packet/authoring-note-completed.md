# Storyworld fixture authoring packet completed

I prepared a repo-ready creative-authoring packet containing the four required F1 fixtures, four additional campaign fixtures, and one additional property recovered from earlier project work.

The repository is intentionally still contract-first and pre-implementation. Its contract workspace explicitly reserves golden-fixture directories for Stillhouse, editorial, BeKindRewind, and commerce, and requires those fixtures to be rights-safe and owner-supplied.    The F1 roadmap likewise requires those four fixture families to round-trip without contaminating the generic narrative model with mandatory commerce or runtime concerns.

I also preserved the repository’s existing template distinctions: Notes to My Daughter and Living Inheritance contribute the illustrated-editorial pattern; Proper Manhood contributes the field-manual pattern; and Dumpster Fire Friends contributes the collectible-character-card pattern.

## The Stillhouse canon boundary

I deliberately did **not** silently declare a final production answer to the Stillhouse mystery.

The packet distinguishes:

* Established property canon: June, 4/17, Route 17, the archive box, the unnamed returning narrator, places remembering, and the recurring visual motifs.
* Fixture canon: the exact near-drowning, June’s survival, family erasure, transfer, character identities, evidence texts, and reveal sequence.
* Production canon: unchanged unless you separately promote the fixture resolution.

That gives the engineer enough raw truth to test the reveal graph while preserving your freedom to resolve the actual published series differently.

## Additional property identified

**The Lanterns** is the one additional property I believe should enter the golden corpus now.

It was absent from the repository’s recovered concept map, which otherwise names the established interactive, mystery, editorial, field-manual, collectible-card, commerce, and adjacent concepts.  Earlier project work contains enough Lanterns canon to make it materially useful: the premise, Kael Vire, his core lie, multidimensional cosmology, Catholic-compatibility constraints, ten episode titles, four audience-belief phases, and the finale recognition.

I classified it as an **extended post-F1 fixture**, not an F1 blocker. It adds something the existing corpus lacks: world rules, character self-deception, moral agency, and safety constraints that govern generation without themselves becoming fictional facts.

I did not add umbrella brands, software concepts, or adjacent media ideas that lacked a sufficiently distinct original narrative world and repeatable content structure.
