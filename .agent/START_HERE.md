## New to Storyworld?

Start with the root README's canonical
[“What is Storyworld?” explanation](../README.md#what-is-storyworld), including
the project's current alpha status. Then use the reading order below to move
from that product overview into the contributor and operator materials.

One boundary is especially important for internal work: suggestions and draft
assets remain proposals until a person approves what becomes official or
public.

## Reading order

1. Current user and platform instructions.
2. Applicable `AGENTS.md` files.
3. `.agent/policy.json` and `.agent/context.json`.
4. `.agent/state/current.json`.
5. Relevant accepted decisions and active task records.
6. `.agent/project.json`, applicable extensions, and validators.
7. Direct inspection of the affected implementation.
8. `project-dossier/README.md` for intended and observed project information.
